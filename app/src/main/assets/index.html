First time-out falls due at 48 h and is overdue after 72 h. Empirical courses with no time-out turn purple and flash at 72 h until someone acknowledges them, and the phone sends a notification. After a decision to continue or change, the next review falls due when the planned days end. Patients are identified by bed number and a code name only; the key linking code names to patients is kept offline by the unit lead. Data is saved on this device only. This tool supports, and does not replace, the hospital antibiotic policy.
