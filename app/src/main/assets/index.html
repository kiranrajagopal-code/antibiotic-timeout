Time-out is due by 72 h (24 h for prophylaxis). Empirical courses at 72 h and prophylaxis at 24 h without a time-out turn purple and flash until someone acknowledges them, and the phone sends a notification. When an antibiotic is switched at a time-out, the old one is stopped and the new one starts its own record, linked to the old. Patients are identified by bed number and a code name only; the key linking code names to patients is kept offline by the unit lead. Data is saved on this device only — use Backup regularly. This tool supports, and does not replace, the hospital antibiotic policy.
